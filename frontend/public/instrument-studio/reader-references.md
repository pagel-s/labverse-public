# Microplate reader reference study

Inspected the [BMG LABTECH CLARIOstar Plus product page](https://www.bmglabtech.com/en/clariostar-plus/) and its [front product photograph](https://www.bmglabtech.com/hubfs/Products/microplate-reader-clariostar-plus.png) before building, 5 October 2026.

Reference envelope: 450 mm wide × 510 mm deep × 400 mm high. Photo cues retained: two silver-white shells, slim purple seam, lower-left rectangular carrier aperture, black slide-out carrier, small indicator lights, upper-right grip. Brand lettering omitted. Original procedural geometry; source photos are not redistributed.

The 96-well plate is a generic demonstration asset. Tray travel, internal cavity, grip construction and rear details are approximate. Extend/retract, load/unload and run/pause/stop are interactive. Loading requires an extended tray; reading requires a loaded, retracted tray; the tray is locked during the illustrative cycle. There is no real measurement, calibration, thermal simulation or hardware control.
