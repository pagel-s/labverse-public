# Plate transport reference study

Reference inspected before building, 5 October 2026: [source](https://www.universal-robots.com/media/1807464/ur3e_e-series_datasheets_web.pdf).

UR3e manufacturer photograph and dimension drawing inspected. 128 mm base, approximately 500 mm reach, metallic links and blue joint caps. Parallel gripper and two stands are original generic fixtures. Connected base/shoulder/elbow/wrist assemblies with 243.5 mm and 213.2 mm links. Analytical two-link positioning and a compensating wrist maintain the gripper pose during a lifted transfer between two stations. Plate ownership follows pick/place state. This is a constrained demonstration, not a validated six-axis robot, collision planner or hardware controller.

Original unbranded procedural geometry, with no reference photograph embedded. Interactions are illustrative and do not reproduce experimental protocols, measurements or real hardware control.
