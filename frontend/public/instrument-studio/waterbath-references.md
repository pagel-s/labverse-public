# Unstirred water bath reference

Viewed the uncovered JBN12 photo on the [Grant JB Nova product page](https://usa.grantinstruments.com/products/jb-nova-unstirred-water-baths). The [12-litre technical drawing](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0944/7027/5420/files/6489a28e0066a0282e3cbfcf_JBA12-JBN12-SAP12-Technical-Datasheet-31622v1_4818a313-a432-471f-a008-244b143cfdf1.pdf?v=1759744490) lists a 360 × 380 × 225 mm base and approximately 323 × 298 mm opening. The open steel tank, white cabinet, front controller and drain fitting are reproduced approximately. The clear pitched cover follows the listed polycarbonate accessory type, with original approximate geometry.

Water bath chosen for the core 'heating block or water bath' slot. A neutral rack and water surface can be added; the shortened demo requires water and a fitted cover. No heating physics, biological processing or temperature recommendation is provided. Source photography is not bundled.
