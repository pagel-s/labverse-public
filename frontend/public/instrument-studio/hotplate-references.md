# Magnetic hotplate stirrer reference

Viewed the main photograph on the [IKA C-MAG HS 7 digital product page](https://www.ika.com/en/Products-LabEq/Magnetic-Stirrers-pg188/C-MAG-HS-7-digital-3487000/), with dimensions from its linked [technical data](https://www.ika.com/en/Products-LabEq/Magnetic-Stirrers-pg188/C-MAG-HS-7-digital-3487000/Technical-Data-cptd.html). The reference uses a 180 × 180 mm ceramic plate and a 215 × 330 × 105 mm base. The raised blue console, two knobs and separation beneath the ceramic top informed this original unbranded model.

A generic beaker and stirring bar demonstrate motion. Heat is an indicator, followed by a three-second illustrative cooldown; neither represents actual temperature. Prop removal is blocked during motion or heat/cool indication. No source photography is bundled.
