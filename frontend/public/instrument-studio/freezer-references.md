# Upright freezer reference

Viewed the open MDF-U334 photograph and dimension drawing in the [PHCbi Biomedical Freezers brochure](https://www.phchd.com/global/-/media/biomedical/global/catalogues/MDF-biomedical/1002-2018-04-AA_PHCbi_Biomedical-Freezers.pdf). Its narrow upright cabinet, five storage drawers, thick single door, interior ribs, external latch and top controller informed the model. Listed external dimensions are 614 × 709 × 1620 mm.

Original unbranded geometry. Small fittings and drawer interiors are approximate. The middle drawer can extend, exposing a neutral inventory box; closing is blocked until it retracts. Alarm demonstration is manually triggered and does not represent an actual temperature failure. Source photos are not bundled.

Validation: 14 state assertions passed; browser checked doors, inventory access and model appearance. Console had no errors or warnings.
