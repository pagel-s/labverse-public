# Cell counter reference study

Inspected the exterior front/rear photographs on page 7 and dimensions on page 54 of the [Countess 3 Automated Cell Counter User Guide](https://documents.thermofisher.com/TFS-Assets/LSG/manuals/MAN0019566_Countess_3_Auto_Cell_Counter_UG.pdf), 5 October 2026. Reference envelope: 9.6 W × 6.7 D × 9.4 H inches (approximately 244 × 170 × 239 mm).

Original unbranded geometry follows the rounded white shell, dark inclined display face, purple trim, front USB socket, slide port and rear vents/service panel. The exact screen inclination, radii and small fittings are estimated from the photos. No source imagery or manufacturer interface is embedded.

The slide is a generic illustrative prop. Insert/eject, start/pause/stop and completion states are provided. Ejection is blocked while imaging, including pause. This is a handling simulation without cell analysis, sample preparation instructions or measured results.
