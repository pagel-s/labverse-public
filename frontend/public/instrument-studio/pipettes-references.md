# Manual pipette reference

Viewed the Research plus photograph and close-up volume windows on printed page 16 (PDF page 46) of the [Eppendorf 2024 catalog](https://www.eppendorf.com/media/MAIN/00-General/Catalog/Product-Catalog-2024/Eppendorf-Maincatalog_2024_INT.pdf). This informed the thumb plunger, ridged adjustment collar, finger hook, display aperture and ejector sleeve. Three colour accents distinguish the variants without branding.

Bodies are approximately 250 mm with tips; dimensions and the custom three-place presentation stand are approximate. The controls select a pipette, attach/eject its tip and change an abstract liquid indicator. Dial values have no physical units and are not calibration. Geometry is original code; source photos are not bundled.

Validation: 19 assertions cover isolated tip/fill states, empty-tip checks and ejection guards. Browser verified attachment, filling and guarded ejection; geometry reviewed.
