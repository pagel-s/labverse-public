# Analytical balance reference

Viewed the close-up and full instrument photographs on the second PDF page of the [Sartorius Entris II brochure](https://www.sartorius.com/download/928708/entris-ii-laboratory-balances-brochure-en-l-sartorius-pdf-data.pdf). The analytical models have a 219 × 317 × 345 mm envelope and 90 mm pan. The model follows the tall glass shield, narrow supports, sliding side pane and sloped front display, with neutral trim and no company branding.

Original approximate geometry. The virtual weight represents 25 g only in the demonstration state. Tare changes the arithmetic offset, and the shield must be closed for a reading. No airflow, settling time, accuracy or calibration is modelled. Reference photography is not included.
