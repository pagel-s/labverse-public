Inspected the product and controller photographs in the [PHCbi MPR-S500H / S500RH flyer](https://www.phchd.com/eu/-/media/biomedical/europe/Marketing_materials/Product_flyers/Preservation/pharmaceutical-refrigerators/sliding-door-refrigerators/MPR-S500H-S500RH/MPR-S500%28R%29H-PE_EN_LR-111125.pdf?rev=-1). The wire-shelf S500H variant informed the twin sliding glass doors, dark perimeter frames, top controls, six shelves and bottom machinery cover. Reference dimensions: 900 × 650 × 1824 mm.

Original unbranded geometry with approximate small fittings. Only one side can be accessible at a time because the doors overlap. Three neutral bottle props can be removed through the left opening. Light and alarm controls demonstrate states; no cooling, storage suitability or preservation outcome is calculated. Photos are not bundled.
