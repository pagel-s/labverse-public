# Microplate sealer reference study

Before building, inspected the [OIST laboratory photograph of a PX1](https://www.oist.jp/image/sqcpx1pcrplatesealer) and the [manufacturer's dimensional specification](https://www.bio-rad.com/de-at/product/px1-pcr-plate-sealer-for-classroom?ID=45eb2a45-3ae4-028e-59f1-a9e0d6ac2307), 5 October 2026. The manufacturer's large catalogue PDF was inaccessible to local retrieval; it was not used as a viewed reference.

The 192 W × 248 D × 219 H mm reference envelope, cream casing, sloping inset screen and lower drawer informed original unbranded geometry. Drawer travel, interior plate support and foil are approximate. No manufacturer image or interface is embedded.

Load a generic plate and foil through the open drawer. The illustrative cycle requires both and a closed drawer; it locks drawer and loading controls while running or paused. It produces no real heat or validated sealing outcome. Foil removal is a manual demonstration action, not a separate automatic seal-removal instrument.
